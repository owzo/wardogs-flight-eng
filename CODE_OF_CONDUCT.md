# Code of conduct

This project is a small tool for one game and one kind of hardware. The people
who show up here are pilots, tinkerers and the occasional driver author, and
the maintainer wants every one of them to be able to ask, report and
contribute without being talked down to.

## Expected

- Be direct and be kind. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
- Report what you measured, not what you expected; say when you are guessing.
- Respect that the maintainer's time is limited and that "no" is an acceptable
  answer to a feature.
- Keep hardware safety in view: a force-feedback base can hurt a hand. Do not
  encourage anyone to run an untested force path without the precautions in
  the README.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or sustained hostility, in issues, pull
  requests, the wiki, or anywhere the project is discussed.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Pressuring anyone to run code against the game's terms, to bypass its
  anti-cheat, or to extract or share game assets, keys or mappings. The
  project's own rules forbid all of these and they are not up for debate here.
- Spam, advertising, or using the issue tracker for unrelated support.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (@owzo on GitHub) may edit, hide or delete content and may
block accounts that breach this document, with a short public note of the
reason when it is safe to give one. Decisions can be appealed once, privately.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at the e-mail address on
the @owzo GitHub profile, or open an issue if you are comfortable doing so in
public. Reports are read by the maintainer only and are not shared without
your agreement, except where the law requires it.

## Scope

This document applies to the repository, its wiki, its releases, and to
anyone representing the project elsewhere.
